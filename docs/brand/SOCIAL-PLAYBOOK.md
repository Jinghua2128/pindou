## Three short-video scripts

Record the working app, keeping private tabs and invitation tokens out of frame. Use original click sounds or music you have rights to use.

**One more bead — about 12 seconds.** 0–2 s: an empty peg, text “I’ll just place one bead.” 2–7 s: quick cuts of picking colors and placing beads. 7–10 s: reveal Pocket frog and rotate the board. 10–12 s: “Small beads. Big good days. / PinDou.” Caption: “One bead turned into a whole little frog. 🐸 #PinDou #BeadArt #PixelArt”. Use cover 09.

**The co-op friend — about 15 seconds.** 0–3 s: neat bead placement, text “Me: let’s make something cute.” 3–7 s: second cursor moves a bead, text “My friend:”. 7–11 s: spill a tray and move a box. 11–15 s: both add color, end card “Little things, made together.” Caption: “Every co-op table has these two people. Which one are you? #PinDou #CoopGames #CreativePlay”. Use cover 08.

**The tiny reset — about 10 seconds.** 0–2 s: a messy tray, text “A little creative break.” 2–7 s: pick up pink beads and build a small heart with quick cuts. 7–10 s: tidy the table, text “Made a little thing. That’s enough.” Caption: “No masterpiece required. Just a little color. #PinDou #BeadArt #MakeSomething”. Use cover 07.
